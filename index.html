Marylin
Personal shopper

She has played mas since she was fifteen and has done Trinidad, Miami, Crop Over and Notting Hill more times than she will admit to. She was the one everyone messaged at two in the morning asking what to wear, and she got tired of watching women buy a nude that had been matched to somebody else's leg.

She works the shade first and the styling second, and she will tell you when one pair is enough.

She will tell you to
  • Match to your arms and face, not your legs, if your legs run paler
  • Judge shade in daylight, never under shop lights or a phone screen
  • Buy the spare. There is no restocking mid-road
  • Size up if you are between sizes. The net stretches, the waistband does not
And not to
  • Wear a brand new pair for the first time on J'ouvert
  • Buy one pair for a three-day carnival
  • Assume nude means anything. It is an assumption, not a colour
  • Pull them on over shimmer oil before it has dried
